About

I studied AI & Robotics, and I've ended up somewhere between machine learning and hardware. Most of my work is about one question: can we trust this number? That might be a reading from a sensor on an aerospace part, or a pixel in a satellite temperature map.

At Komoline I build the software that calibrates sensors: it measures how far they drift, fits corrections, and checks the result against reference data. At ISRO I worked on the satellite side, training ESRGAN-style models and building the data pipelines that feed them.

Outside work I like ocean dynamics, PyTorch experiments, and making small tools that save someone an afternoon.
